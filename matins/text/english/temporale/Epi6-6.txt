Saturday of the Sixth Week after Epiphany
Feria
[Tempora/Epi6-6]

Lesson I
Lesson from the letter of St. Paul the Apostle to the Hebrews
Heb 13:1-4
Let the charity of the brotherhood abide in you. And hospitality do not forget; for by this some, being not aware of it, have entertained angels. Remember them that are in bands, as if you were bound with them; and them that labour, as being yourselves also in the body. Marriage honourable in all, and the bed undefiled. For fornicators and adulterers God will judge.

℟. Mercy and judgment I will sing to thee, O Lord: * I will sing, and I will understand in the unspotted way, when thou shalt come to me.
℣. I walked in the innocence of my heart, in the midst of my house.
℟. Mercy and judgment I will sing to thee, O Lord

Lesson II
Heb 13:5-8
Let your manners be without covetousness, contented with such things as you have; for he hath said: I will not leave thee, neither will I forsake thee. So that we may confidently say: The Lord is my helper: I will not fear what man shall do to me. Remember your prelates who have spoken the word of God to you; whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation, Jesus Christ, yesterday, and to day; and the same for ever.

℟. Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my crying come unto thee. * For thou wilt not despise, O God, the prayer of the destitute
℣. O let thine ears consider well the voice of my complaint
℟. For thou wilt not despise, O God, the prayer of the destitute.
℣. Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, * and to the Holy Ghost.
℟. For thou wilt not despise, O God, the prayer of the destitute.
