Commemoration of: Vigil of St. Bartholomew, Apostle Continuation of the Holy Gospel according to John
John 15:12-16
In that time Jesus said to his disciples: This is my commandment, that you love one another, as I have loved you. And so on.

Homily by Pope St Gregory (the Great.)
21th on the Gospels
All the holy words of the Lord are full of His commandments. Why, then, speaketh the Lord of the commandment to love one another as if He gave no other commandment? This, saith He, is My commandment, That ye love one another. Is it not because love is the one object of all His commandments, and all His commandments are one? For, even as a tree, having but one root, bringeth forth many branches, so, if the root be love, many virtues do spring therefrom. Neither is the branch of good works green, if it abide not in the root of love.
